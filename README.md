# RuMLeEx Website

A responsive black-and-gold website prototype for RuMLeEx, with **LEARN • EXPLORE** as the leading identity and RUN as a supporting pillar.

## Sections
- About RuMLeEx
- SAFAR — stories of people and journeys in progress
- RuMLeEx पाठशाला — lessons from people who have built something meaningful, regardless of age or retirement status
- Explore
- RuMLeEx Store preview — merchandise first, affiliate recommendations and possible future digital resources
- Media services and contact

## Preview locally
Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## Before publishing
1. Replace `hello@rumleex.com` with a verified business email.
2. Add approved YouTube and social profile links.
3. Add the official RuMLeEx logo and approved photographs.
4. Replace placeholder content with real episodes, products and service details.
5. Confirm domain ownership and connect the domain to the hosting provider.
6. Add privacy, terms, shipping, returns and other applicable policies before enabling commerce.

## Important limitations
This is a front-end prototype. The Store is a presentation section only: there is no shopping cart, payment gateway, inventory, shipping, order management or digital download delivery. The contact link uses a placeholder email until replaced.
